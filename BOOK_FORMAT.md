# How every book is shown on the channel

Every book follows the same pattern. Viewers learn what to expect, and production becomes a repeatable process.

## 1. The package for each book

| Piece | How many | When it's published | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trailer Short** | 1 | 3 days before Ep. 1 | "New story starts Friday" builds anticipation |
| **Episodes** | 4–8 (one per chapter group, 15–40 min each) | Once a week, same day and time | Main watch time |
| **Shorts** | 3–5 per episode | Daily between episodes | New viewers, then they move to episodes |
| **"Explained in 10 minutes"** | 1 | After the final episode | Reaches people who don't want the whole story |
| **Full sleep edition** | 1 | One week after the final episode | Long-term watch hours, gets views for years |
| **Community post / poll** | 2–3 | During the series | "Would you have waited?" engagement between uploads |

## 2. How to split a book into episodes

1. Use the book's own natural breaks (nights, chapters, letters, a change of narrator).
2. Aim for **15–40 minutes per episode** (about 2,000–5,500 words at 150 words per minute).
3. **End every episode on a question or a turn**, never in a calm moment.
4. A very short final section (like the Morning in *White Nights*) becomes the finale with your longest reflection.

## 3. Structure inside every episode

| Time | Part | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | **Hook** | Your own words: a striking fact or a quote and a question. No "welcome to my channel". |
| 0:30–1:00 | **"Previously…"** | A 20-second recap (skip in Ep. 1). The series title card with the moon or progress mark. |
| 1:00– | **Narration** | The real text, with the visual code: present, memory, dream, thought. |
| Every 4–6 min | **Storytelling break** | 20–40 seconds of your own insight: context, character, "notice this…". |
| Last 1–2 min | **Outro** | A teaser for the next episode and one emotional question for the comments. The end screen goes to the next episode. |

## 4. Shared visual identity, adapted per book

**Always the same:** logo, title font, the narrator's voice, the gold quote style, captions, the end-screen layout, the "living painting" style.

**Changes with each book:** a color palette and one symbol that tracks progress through the series.

| Book | Palette | Progress symbol |
|---|---|---|
| *White Nights* | Night blue + gold | Moon filling up |
| *Notes from Underground* | Dark green + candle orange | Candle burning down |
| *The Metamorphosis* (Kafka) | Grey + sickly yellow | Apple on the floor |
| *The Overcoat* (Gogol) | Snow white + dark brown | The coat being sewn |
| *The Death of Ivan Ilyich* (Tolstoy) | Sepia + pale light | A window getting brighter |
| Poe stories | Black + deep red | A clock |

## 5. Channel layout

- **One playlist per book**, plus a "Start Here: Best Stories" playlist, a "Sleep Editions" playlist and a "Shorts" playlist.
- Channel trailer: 60 seconds of the best moments from several books.
- **Featured video:** the most-watched episode 1 of any series.
- **Book order:** alternate famous titles (search traffic) with hidden gems (curiosity). Group books by mood ("lonely Russians", "dark tales", "love stories") so viewers move from one book to the next.

## 6. Production checklist for each new book

- [ ] Confirm the text and the translation are public domain (author died more than 70 years ago, and a translation published before 1930)
- [ ] Split into episodes and pull 15+ quotable lines for Shorts
- [ ] Write hooks, breaks and outros for each episode
- [ ] Choose a palette and progress symbol; make a title card and a thumbnail template
- [ ] Record the narration and mark where storytelling breaks go
- [ ] Edit episodes; cut the Shorts
- [ ] Schedule: trailer, then weekly episodes with daily Shorts, then the explainer and the sleep edition
