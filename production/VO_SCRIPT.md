# Voice-over recording script: White Nights, Episode 1 samples

Record each line as its own file, named as shown (for example `vo/long/seg_00.wav`). Then re-render with
`python3 render.py long --vo vo/long`. The video timing, captions and music ducking follow your recording automatically.

**Tips:** warm and unhurried. Leave about 0.5 s of silence before and after each line. Lines in *italics* are the book's text (Constance Garnett translation). Lines in plain text are the channel's own words: say them more personally, like talking to a friend.
